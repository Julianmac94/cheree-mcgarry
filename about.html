About Cheree

“Real change happens when people feel truly heard — not fixed, not advised. Just met.”

Cheree McGarry

With over 25 years of experience in counselling and wellness, Cheree offers a grounded, compassionate presence that meets you exactly where you are. Her approach is warm, collaborative, and deeply human.

Cheree is an Accredited Mental Health Social Worker (AMHSW) and a registered member of the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW). She works with individuals, couples, children, and families — in person at her Karalee practice, or online.
