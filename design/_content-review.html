Funding insight panels + Step 3 outcome  —  Individual therapy
Individual + Medicare (MHCP) → Request form
Price shown$195 / session
Out of pocket$107.75 after $87.25 Medicare rebate
Before booking: GP or psychiatrist Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) naming Cheree McGarry — AMHSW is required before your first session.
Key points shown
  • Up to 10 sessions per calendar year — GP review required after the first 6
  • Rebate of $87.25 claimed via Services Australia — or ask Cheree to submit on your behalf (case-by-case)
  • Cannot claim both Medicare and private health for the same session
  • Medicare rebates cannot be applied to cancellation fees

Child+Medicare (MHCP)→ Request form
Price shown$195 / session
Out of pocket$107.75 after $87.25 rebate
Before booking: A parent or guardian intake with Cheree is required first. Then a GP or psychiatrist MHCP naming Cheree McGarry — AMHSW for the child.
  • One parent/guardian intake required — both parents welcome to attend together
  • Bring the child's and a parent/guardian's Medicare card (required for children under 14)
  • Up to 10 sessions per calendar year — GP review required after the first 6
  • Parent intake may also be claimable under Medicare — discuss with your GP
