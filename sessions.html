Ready to get started?

One-on-one

Individual Therapy

A space that belongs entirely to you — no agenda, no timeline, no right or wrong way to show up.

Your first session is mostly a conversation. Cheree gets to know you — what’s brought you here, what feels heavy, and what you’re hoping might shift. You don’t need to have it figured out before you arrive. In fact, most people find the first session helps them work out what they actually want to focus on.

From there, sessions follow your lead. Some people are working through something specific: anxiety, grief, burnout, relationship stress, a big life change. Others come because something just isn’t right and they can’t quite name it yet. Both are completely valid starting points.

Suits Anyone navigating something personal — at any stage of life

Coverage options

  • Medicare (Better Access)
  • Private / self-funded
  • NDIS — self or plan-managed
  • QFES Employee Assistance
  • ADFHSC & DVA

Together

Couple or Family Therapy

When something feels stuck between you — having someone outside it can help you hear each other differently.

Sessions usually begin with each person having the chance to share their perspective — not to argue the case, but so Cheree can understand the full picture. From there the work becomes collaborative: practising different ways of communicating, working through something that’s caused distance, or simply building something stronger between you.

This works for romantic partners, co-parents, adult children and their parents, siblings, or any close relationship that could use some support. You don’t need to be in crisis — some couples and families come simply because they want to.

Each person completes a brief individual intake session before joint sessions begin.
Suits Partners, co-parents, families, adult children & parents, siblings

Coverage options

  • Private / self-funded
  • QFES Employee Assistance
  • ADFHSC & DVA

Medicare and NDIS are not available for couple or family sessions.

For children

Child Counselling (under 16)

Children carry a lot — and they don’t always have words for it. Sessions are designed to meet them where they are.

Cheree uses age-appropriate approaches — some conversational, some more activity-based, depending on the child and what they need. The goal is always to create a space where they feel at ease and engaged, not studied or managed.

Parents and guardians are an important part of the process. An initial intake session with you comes first — to understand the background, share what you’ve observed, and shape how the sessions will run. Both parents are welcome to come together for the intake.

Suits Children experiencing anxiety, behavioural challenges, school pressures, family changes, grief, or anything else that seems to be weighing on them

Coverage options

  • Medicare (Better Access)
  • Private / self-funded
  • NDIS — self or plan-managed
  • QFES Employee Assistance
  • ADFHSC & DVA

Facilitated resolution

Professional Mediation

Not every dispute needs a courtroom — sometimes it just needs a skilled, neutral person in the room.

Mediation is a structured, confidential process. Cheree facilitates dialogue between two parties to help them reach a workable agreement — without the cost, stress, or adversarial nature of formal proceedings. The outcome isn’t decided by Cheree; it’s reached by you, with her help.

It works best when both parties genuinely want resolution — not necessarily to agree on everything, but to reach something both can move forward from. An individual intake session with each party comes first.

Suits Workplace conflicts, family disputes, or any situation where an independent facilitator can help two parties reach agreement

Coverage options

  • Private / self-funded
  • QFES Employee Assistance
  • ADFHSC & DVA

Medicare and NDIS are not available for mediation services.

Coverage & costs

How sessions are paid for

There’s more than one way to access sessions with Cheree. Here’s what each option actually means for you.

Private / Self-funded

$195 / session

The most straightforward path — no referral, no GP visit, no waiting. Book directly and pay on the day. A tax invoice is issued immediately. If you have private health extras, check with your insurer about potential rebates.

Available for all session types.

Medicare — Better Access

~$107.75 out of pocket

See your GP and get a Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) naming Cheree McGarry (AMHSW). You can then claim a $87.25 Medicare rebate per session, bringing your cost to around $107.75. Up to 10 sessions per calendar year, with a GP review after the first 6.

Individual and child therapy only.

NDIS

Self or plan-managed

If counselling is in your NDIS plan, you may be able to use your funding. Self-managed: pay on the day ($195) and claim reimbursement. Plan-managed: invoiced directly to your plan manager at the NDIS rate. Confirm counselling is in your funded supports before booking.

Individual and child therapy only.

QFES — Employee Assistance

Covered by QFES

Queensland Fire & Emergency Services employees can access sessions through the EAP at no cost to you. Contact your EAP coordinator to confirm your entitlement and arrange a referral before booking. Couple, family, and mediation sessions may also be covered — check with your coordinator.

QFES employees only.

ADFHSC & DVA

Covered by funder

Current and former ADF members, their families, and DVA card holders may be eligible for funded sessions billed directly to ADFHSC or DVA. Contact your unit health officer, Medical Officer, or DVA case manager to arrange a referral before booking.

ADF members, veterans & families.
