My story

How I got
to here

I came to this work from life, not just training. I’ve personally navigated a lot — the kind of things that are only really understood from the inside. That lived experience sits alongside my formal background in ways I couldn’t have planned, and I think it’s part of what makes this work feel real rather than clinical.

My background spans many different roles and settings — organisations, workplaces, community contexts, private practice. I’ve worked across mediation, training, and professional supervision. All of it shapes how I approach things now: with flexibility, with curiosity, and a genuine interest in the person in front of me.

What grounds me is social work. Unlike psychology, social work doesn’t start from the assumption that difficulty belongs to the individual. It understands people as part of families, communities, cultures and systems — all of it shaping each other. I find that honest. People aren’t problems to be solved. They’re navigating a world that is genuinely complex.

I’ve been doing this for over 25 years. I’m still learning. I still find it meaningful.
