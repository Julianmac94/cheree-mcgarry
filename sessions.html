01

What type of support are you looking for?

One-on-one

Individual Therapy

For individuals dealing with anxiety, stress, depression, grief, relationship difficulties, life transitions, or simply wanting to feel better and understand themselves more.

Together

Couple or Family Therapy

For partners or families struggling to communicate, feeling disconnected, or navigating a crisis together. Also suitable for parents & adult children, siblings, or blended families.

Individual intake for each person required first

For children

Child (under 16)

For young people experiencing anxiety, behavioural difficulties, school pressures, or difficult life changes. Sessions are age-appropriate and parents play an active role throughout.

Parent or guardian intake session required first

Facilitated process

Professional Mediation

For workplace conflicts, family disputes, or any situation where two parties need a neutral, impartial facilitator to help reach a workable resolution.

Individual intake session required first

How are your sessions covered?

Medicare

$87.25 rebate per session · Requires a GP Mental Health Care Plan · Individual & child only

Private / Self-funded

$195 per session · No referral or GP visit needed · Most flexible option · Private health extras may apply

NDIS — Self-managed

You pay & claim back · $195 per session · Must have counselling in your funded supports

NDIS — Plan-managed

Your plan manager pays directly · Pre-approval required · Must have counselling in your funded supports

QFES

Free to you · Queensland Fire & Emergency Services employees only · EAP referral required

ADFHSC & DVA

Free to you · Current & former ADF members, their families, and DVA card holders · Referral required
